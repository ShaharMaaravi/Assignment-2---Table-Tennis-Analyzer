How should these metrics be interpreted?

Ball speed is measured in pixels per second, not km/h. Real-world speed requires camera calibration and court-plane perspective correction.

Estimated hits use stable horizontal ball-direction reversals and should be treated as an approximation when detections are missed.

Momentum combines ball direction and speed, rally duration, movement efficiency, and available score state. It is an explainable analytical index—not win probability.
